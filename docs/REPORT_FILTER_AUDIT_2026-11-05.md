## 1. Method

One seeded world whose every figure and name is a string that appears nowhere else (`backend/tests/Feature/ReportFiltersFunctionalTest.php`: two ad accounts of one project, Snapchat sales and Meta leads; two days inside October, one day in the window before, one day after; one creative per campaign). «Hidden» is then a substring search of the whole serialised answer of each surface, never of a list the test happens to know about.

Surfaces read for every option: the shared page (`GET /reports/shared/{token}`), the live payload (`…/live`), the platform drawers (`…/live/platform/{provider}`), the content library, its summary, the widgets and the attribution answers (`…/creatives`, `…/creatives/summary`, `…/widgets`, `…/attribution`), the print document (`GET /reports/print/{token}`, the PDF's data), CSV and XLSX (`…/download/{format}`), the builder preview (`GET projects/{p}/reports/{report}`), and the stored copy itself (`reports.data`). The source filters (accounts, projects, providers, campaigns) are held by `ReportSourceIsolationTest` under REPORT-SOURCE-ISOLATION-001 and are not repeated here.

«Before» is the branch base `92c2969fc`; «after» is this branch. 8 of the 12 tests fail on the base.

Allowed statuses: NOT_STARTED, IN_PROGRESS, PARTIAL, IMPLEMENTED_NOT_VERIFIED, VERIFIED, BLOCKED_EXTERNAL_CREDENTIALS, BLOCKED_OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE. Every row below is at most IMPLEMENTED_NOT_VERIFIED: the suite passes locally on the worktree's own database, nothing is merged, deployed or seen on Production.

## 3. The open points, decided and closed (Owner, 2026-11-05)

Owner decision: «ظلت مقاييس/أعمدة غير مختارة في payload/الطباعة؛ يجب إتمام قطعها من الخادم، وفحص أرقام المقارنة ومصادر الإسناد والمصادر المختارة، لا الاكتفاء بإخفائها بصريًا.»

1. **Unchosen KPI values and unchosen column values — cut on the server.** One function, `ChosenFigures` (`backend/app/Domains/Reports/Support/ChosenFigures.php`), at the serving boundary beside `HiddenMoney` and `PreviousComparison::strip`: `PublicReportController::show` (snapshot page), `::live`, the content, widget and attribution answers, `LiveDrilldown` (platform and content drawers) and `ReportPrintController::data` (the PDF's document, client and executive audiences). `reports.data` is never written. Only a stated choice cuts (the link's list, a picked automatic composition, the report's `scope.metrics`, a customised `config.metric_set`; a link's own columns, else the report's); an objective's default template is nobody's choice, so a report with no list is served byte for byte as before (`LiveReportPayloadIdentityTest` unchanged; `ReportExistingSettingsUnchangedTest`: the four entries with no list unchanged, the three that carry a list rewritten — `live_narrowed`, `live_narrowed_drawer`, `snapshot_link`). Cards and charts that read an unchosen key lose it with it: a row that is the figure leaves its list, a block named by it takes the silent shape its reader already draws as nothing, a funnel stage loses its rate from an unchosen stage, Results and Leads take their «not configured» shape, a store stage of an unchosen amount leaves the funnel. The client page, the deck and the PDF draw no block of a figure the answer does not carry (`frontend/src/features/reports/chosenFigures.ts`: spend split and spend lines, the platform cards' spend line and share card, a content sort by an absent figure, the funnel's cost per stage, a CTR column of dashes, the PDF's ad spend column, creative chips); a table nobody restricted follows the KPI list. Rendered against the server's real cut answers (deck, PDF document, the five live views): no `NaN`, no zero standing for an absent figure.
2. **The two comparisons built from their own read — removed with the switch.** `PreviousComparison::strip` drops the attention finding whose impact is `extra_cost_vs_previous_rate`, removes `before` and `change` from every other finding's KPIs, and drops the stored note of kind `period_comparison` (a third comparison found while proving this one: «بفارق 75% عن الفترة السابقة», carried with `change`). The content section reads its rows without `previous` while the switch is off (`PreviousComparison::stripCreativeRow` in `SharedCreativeView` library, summary, detail and comparison), so the pulse's «fastest growing» and «declining» and every against-itself finding are empty by construction; `previous_period` leaves the summary, its insights and the detail; a fatigue signal keeps its direction and loses the figure it compared with.
3. **Store, GA4 and attribution sources** re-proved with seeded rows (row 30). One defect found and fixed: a link naming one ad platform of two (no account, no campaign) was not «narrower than its project», so the attribution answer published the whole store against it; `ReportShare::narrowerThanItsProject` now compares the link's platforms with those the project's active accounts ran on.
3a. **Correction after the wider suite (same day).** The first cut (`a7e7b7869`) broke 8 tests outside the filter it was run with. Every one of those fixtures states a choice, so the trigger was right and the cut was wrong in four ways, now fixed: (i) the operator's preview and the files were not cut while the page and the PDF were — the four surfaces disagreed; `ReportSectionSurfaces::previewPayload` and `ReportExporter::exportData` now pass through the same `ChosenFigures::cut`; (ii) a sentence with no statement of a figure (an operator's approved note) was dropped fail-closed — only a sentence that states an unchosen figure (`reveals`, `metric`, or a KPI label) leaves now; (iii) the Results block and a content row's `verified_result` were treated as KPI cards — they are defined by the link's result events (`result_events`, `results_off`), their own stated control, and stay; only a cost per result leaves, with spend; (iv) two assertions read an unchosen figure of a fixture that states a list and were updated: `LiveLinkBeforeCampaignsTest` (spend is absent, not null, on a link that chose impressions and clicks) and `ReportResultStageTruthTest` (its link now chooses impressions, whose stage it reads). The settings baseline moved for the two files of the report that restricts `days` (`snapshot_file`, `saved_file`) as well.
4. **The stored `links` block** has no switch and no account bound (unchanged; `ChosenFigures` leaves it as it is).
5. **The duplicate flow** was not audited here; `fix/report-duplicate-draft` changes it to prefill → editor draft → publish and carries its own containment test.
6. **Left open, stated rather than decided here:** an attention finding still names a movement in its headline («ارتفعت تكلفة العميل المحتمل») with the comparison off — no figure of the window before travels, the sentence does; the live payload's `period.previous` dates and its «مقابل الفترة السابقة» label are dates and words, not figures; a row's identity (`provider` on an account or content row) stays when the `platform` column is not chosen; content rows carry `ratio_inputs` pairs that state spend on a link hiding spend (a HiddenMoney gap seen in the `live_narrowed` baseline, outside this row, raised as its own task).

## 4. Absent on main and not invented

- A **report timezone filter**: `reports.timezone` exists as a column and is echoed by `ReportController`, but no option sets it and no predicate reads it for a report.
- An **attribution-window option**: `reports.attribution_window` is a label printed by `ReportExporter`; nothing bounds a figure by it.
- A **UTM section**: no section, no predicate (the only `utm` reference is a name sanitiser inside `GenericNames`).
- A **report-level currency field**: `ReportController::store` pins `currency` to `ReportingCurrency::DEFAULT`; the stated currency is derived from the rows (row 17).
